## Direct answer: what does a combined c + x budget mean?

For one rider and one ETA coordinate at revision `j`, let `v_j` be the last actually published promise, `e_j` the kept-route projection under current conditions, and `p_j` the candidate projection:

`x_j=e_j−v_j`, `c_j=p_j−e_j`, `z_j=p_j−v_j=x_j+c_j`.

| Proposed accumulated quantity | Meaning and existing equivalence |
|---|---|
| `C_n=Σ|c_j|` | Existing C: irreversible decision-induced variation; the traffic/progress term is excluded. |
| `L_n=Σ(x_j+c_j)` | Signed displacement. Since `v_j=p_(j−1)`, `L_n=p_n−p_0`. Checking `L_n≤B` at every evaluated prefix reproduces **Mplus-B**, with the same projections, epochs, anchor, and recovery rules. Calling it cumulative does not make it path-dependent. A terminal-only check would be weaker. |
| `V_n=Σ|x_j+c_j|` | Existing V: total publication-to-publication variation, charging advances as well as delays. It already combines both causes before taking the absolute value. |
| `T_n=Σ(|x_j|+|c_j|)` | Different, stricter accounting: `T_n≥V_n` by the triangle inequality. It charges changes canceled within an epoch. |
| `H_n=Σmax(0,x_j+c_j)` | Different irreversible deterioration budget. Advances create no credit; unlike Mplus, returning to an earlier promise does not restore spent budget. This is a candidate variant, not a demonstrated novel principle. |

For a **hypothetical** revision with `x=+60 s` and `c=−60 s`, the combined visible increment is zero, while separated absolute accounting charges 120 s. Thus “count both” is insufficient to define the contract.

Implementation evidence: [PromiseDeltaCalculator.cs:137](E:/Code/RideBound-deadline/src/RideBound.Application/Promises/PromiseDeltaCalculator.cs:137) takes absolute ETA differences; [CommitmentLedger.cs:285](E:/Code/RideBound-deadline/src/RideBound.Domain/Commitments/CommitmentLedger.cs:285) accumulates decision-induced or visible deltas. [C-30.json:6,23](E:/Code/Report_INT3508/ridebound-scratchpad/tang3/configs/C-30.json:6) and [V-30.json:6,23](E:/Code/Report_INT3508/ridebound-scratchpad/tang3/configs/V-30.json:6) select those bases and caps. The deadline compares candidate and initial drop times ([CommitmentLockEvaluator.cs:186](E:/Code/RideBound-deadline/src/RideBound.Domain/Commitments/CommitmentLockEvaluator.cs:186)). The canonical budget vector cannot directly store signed values ([CommitmentVector.cs:33](E:/Code/RideBound-deadline/src/RideBound.Domain/Commitments/CommitmentVector.cs:33)).

**An unavoidable limit:** on continuation without insertion, `p=e`, hence `c=0` and `z=x`. Traffic alone can exhaust V, H, T, or a deadline. Rejecting insertion cannot repair that state; recovery and overrides must remain visible ([CommitmentLockEvaluator.cs:156](E:/Code/RideBound-deadline/src/RideBound.Domain/Commitments/CommitmentLockEvaluator.cs:156)). Moreover, actual arrival obeys `A−p_0=(p_n−p_0)+(A−p_n)`; an endpoint projection cap does not eliminate the last execution residual. Keep operator accountability and experienced reliability as separate measurements even when both influence admission.

### If the first promise is forecast-aware

Let `F_0=P_0+b_0`, where `b_0` is an explicit forecast/padding extension.

- **Fixed padding applied consistently:** add the same rider-specific `b_0` to every `v,e,p` and to the initial anchor. All increments are unchanged; `p+b_0≤P_0+b_0+B` reduces to `p≤P_0+B`. Display calibration changes, but dispatch flexibility does not.
- **Only the internal anchor is extended:** comparing naive `p` against `F_0+B` permits `p≤P_0+b_0+B`. This is a padded Mplus deadline. Later display reversion to naive times would charge an artificial correction; a separately defined persistent display can avoid that reversion. The internal gate and external promise must be stated explicitly.
- **Forecast recomputed at every publication:** use `v=last published D`, `e=D(t,e_naive)`, `p=D(t,p_naive)`, then recompute all three increments. Never transform the historical `v` with today's forecast. Signed increments still telescope; V now includes forecast-update variation as well as other changes. Because `D=max(P,stretch(...))` is nonlinear, old `x,c` cannot simply be copied into this ledger.

Current Family A is display replay, not this online ledger integration ([display_layer.py:3](E:/Code/Report_INT3508/ridebound-scratchpad/optimize/display_layer.py:3); [forecast_family_a.py:84–99](E:/Code/Report_INT3508/ridebound-scratchpad/optimize/forecast_family_a.py:84)). Its extension `D0−P0` is an accounting definition, not independently identified traffic seconds. If deliberate pooling reserve is added, record it separately from forecast extension.

**Recommendation:** keep C as the accountability ledger; optimize promise calibration and an explicit reliability gate alongside it. If the owner instead wants a single irreversibly accumulated customer-visible contract, use existing V or test H against V and Mplus. Do not rename V as a new combined method.

## 3. Optimize the intersection of a C budget and a total-drift deadline

1. **Objective / variable:** maximize service while respecting both `Σ|c|≤B` and `p_j−P0≤S`. Initially hold `B=30 s` and sweep proposed `S∈{30,60,120} s`; retain the same recovery rule. This gives accumulated operator accountability plus an explicit customer deadline that sees x, rather than pretending traffic is controllable.
2. **Curve:** completion versus experienced lateness and variation; compare hybrid, C-only, Mplus-only, and V at matched targets. Increasing S releases deadline restriction while retaining C. Override rates expose traffic-only infeasibility.
3. **Novelty:** intersecting known constraints is not an established new principle. The candidate result is a useful nondominated contract within the specific per-rider ledger/certificate framework. This is preferable to claiming that `Σ(c+x)` differs from Mplus.
4. **Zero-run test:** use the raw T3.8 transcripts and [audit_vep.py](E:/Code/Report_INT3508/ridebound-scratchpad/tang3/evidence/audit_vep.py) to count both predicates on every recorded reference and chosen candidate; compare with the T3.8 jobs and forecast cells above. This can find redundant gates and traffic-only failures. It cannot identify unchosen feasible insertions or simulate changed future routes. Retrospectively passing a gate is not a counterfactual policy result.
5. **Jobs:** **3 hybrid arms×6 cells=18 new jobs** if direction 1 already supplies all controls; otherwise add **6 Mplus-120 control jobs**, giving **24**. Existing C-30/Mplus-30/Mplus-60/V-30 controls are cached in the current world. Verify both are enabled without changing structural fields. 6. **Prediction / kill:** proposed pilot screen: one hybrid reduces actual late share by at least **1 pp** versus C-only and the share exceeding **60 s** visible variation by at least **1 pp** versus matched Mplus, with at most **1 pp** completion loss versus either control and no increase in forced-rider share. These are proposed screening thresholds, not estimated business values. Kill if no hybrid passes, if every hybrid is dominated, if the extra gate never binds usefully, or if traffic-only forced transitions erase the reliability benefit. Stop before adding forecast integration when the basic intersection has no useful frontier.

The ranked plan deliberately separates **accountability optimization**, **promise calibration**, and **physical service capacity**. Proceed from saved-data screens to the smallest discriminating pilot; retain negative results and the precise claim boundary.
