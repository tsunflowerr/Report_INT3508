# Round 10B: ML pilot v2 review

V2 asks a useful bounded question: compare historical-trip prediction with fewer dispatch changes at quote cost. It is not a new general ETA or predictability idea; Ding et al. learn pooled offers from initial fleet state, and Leonardi et al. study simple-input DRT prediction and operating constraints (primary evidence in [our primary-source survey](E:/Code/Report_INT3508/ridebound-scratchpad/optimize/codex-debate/codex-work/SURVEY-ML-ETA-CODEX.md:19)). No model or simulation was run here.

**Numerical spot-check passes; general timing certification does not.** Our [independent code](E:/Code/Report_INT3508/ridebound-scratchpad/optimize/codex-debate/codex-work/round10b_verify_riders_codex.py:1) verifies 15,023 rows/179 runs and the claimed SHA256 (2604de64…c733e), with no duplicate rider-prefix keys. It checks 30 rows across 10 runs: 420 numeric comparisons and 60 causal load/onboard comparisons, zero discrepancies. All checked runs have every promised rider completed. Every frame hash was verified and transcript hashes recorded ([log:3](E:/Code/Report_INT3508/ridebound-scratchpad/optimize/codex-debate/codex-work/round10b-rider-spotcheck-codex.txt:3), [row evidence:2](E:/Code/Report_INT3508/ridebound-scratchpad/optimize/codex-debate/codex-work/round10b-rider-spotcheck-codex.tsv:2)). This validates the selected rows, not all 179 runs. The extractor's load definition uses only eventual completed riders, groups them by final assigned vehicle, and asks whether future alighting is after t0 ([extractor:51–59](E:/Code/Report_INT3508/ridebound-scratchpad/optimize/codex-debate/claude-work/ml_extract_riders.py:51)). Even when counts coincide with causal state, that implementation is not a timing proof. Reconstruct assigned/onboard sets from source-ordered events and applied decisions through the first publication, using its vehicle; include accepted riders whose later outcome is unfinished/cancelled. Equal timestamps do not license reading a later publication.

The CSV contains only completed riders ([extractor:51](E:/Code/Report_INT3508/ridebound-scratchpad/optimize/codex-debate/claude-work/ml_extract_riders.py:51)). The independent [full-panel cross-check:11](E:/Code/Report_INT3508/ridebound-scratchpad/optimize/codex-debate/codex-work/round10b-csv-inventory-crosscheck.json:11) finds accepted=completed and CSV rows=completed for every one of 179 runs: no accepted-rider censoring is evident in this panel. Count agreement does not prove assignment/source-order timing or first-promise completeness; the quote-cost convention remains all first promises. Obtain all-request coverage denominators from the independent inventory. Bin/minute are window-relative, not clock time; convert using window start. direct_s is rounded maxRide/1.5, a service-constraint proxy, not measured current direct travel. wait_promised_s is pickup minus request arrival, not pickup minus publication ([extractor:61](E:/Code/Report_INT3508/ridebound-scratchpad/optimize/codex-debate/claude-work/ml_extract_riders.py:61)). Those quantities can be known at publication, but names and definitions must be explicit. Targets, future sharing, final assignment, future plans and ledger totals must never enter features.

The draft mixes inactive training forecasts on 12/13 with active later forecasts, tunes quantile levels to approximate mean quote cost without a calibration split, and calls the resulting figures matched on test although test costs can drift. It also permits choosing GBM versus fc+GBM after seeing outcomes. Report empirical coverage only: a quantile learner, clipping and subsequent cost adjustment do not guarantee calibrated rider/day coverage. The 0.3 pp ML1 target is below a single-event rate change in important date/window cohorts. ML4 must compare policies in the same world and is a finite-model result. ML5 should remove genuinely vehicle-state features; room is a known request/initial-plan constraint, not realized pooling.

No, the project is not out of useful work. Saved simulated trips support one limited offline comparison of prediction and operational commitment, with the already established service and quote-cost trade-offs kept visible. The contribution can be the audited cumulative revision budget and signed residual accounting combined with a frozen predictor, rather than inventing trip-based ETA learning. A null pilot is useful evidence about these inputs and worlds; it neither proves that ML adds nothing nor bounds real-world gains.

## Exact sealed version I would accept

**Gates and population.** First correct/re-extract causal load and recover every first-published promise, including later unfinished/cancelled riders; preserve completed-only error labels. Seal exact run/config/world/runner/CSV hashes, original source-trip groups, feature definitions and output names. Keep worlds/eras/arms separate, select one effective run per cell, exclude retries and calibration duplicates. Purge fit/calibration source-trip duplicates; absence of recoverable provenance or initial state blocks the pilot.

**Splits.** Measured-factor U and C-30: fit 12/13 w07 r1–r4; calibration 14 w07 r3/r4; test 15 and 16 separately, w07 r1/r2/r4. Other windows/weekends and extra C-30 calibration data are transfer diagnostics, not primary fitting. Static five-arm WP14r ladder: fit day12 r1–r3 in both w08/w17, calibrate day12 r4, test day13 r1–r4. Samples stay grouped. Use a fixed traffic-profile vintage from 12/13 throughout fit/calibration/test; retrospective training transforms are not claims that this profile existed online on 12/13. No test-day future factors are inputs. Already inspected dates remain a retrospective pilot, not a fresh holdout.

**Learners/features.** Use pinned sklearn 1.7.2, no installs. One target (A−D0) in seconds; D0 is the fixed historical display, or P0 for static. Base inputs: clock minute, drop horizon, pickup−t0, forecast extension, maxRide and initial room. Primary adds causal other-assigned and other-onboard counts. Fix HGB loss=quantile, quantile=.90, learning_rate=.05, max_iter=100, max_leaf_nodes=7, min_samples_leaf=20, l2_regularization=1, random_state=7, early_stopping=False; fit identical no-state ablation and standardized linear QuantileRegressor(quantile=.90, alpha=1, solver=highs). No model/quantile search. Future ledger components are diagnostics only.

**Matched cost.** E=15/30/60 seconds total mean extension from P0; primary E=30. Over all calibration first promises, let δ=D0−P0, w=max(0,predicted residual), ρ=(E−meanδ)/meanw; F=D0+ρw. Mark E<meanδ infeasible; zero meanw degenerates to uniform extra padding. Controls P0+E and D0+(E−meanδ) use the same calibration population. Freeze rules before test labels; no test rescaling. Report all-promised test mean/p90 extension (nearest rank, ceiling(0.90n)) and mean horizon, with completed/common-completed costs supplementary. A win requires whole-policy mean cost≤control+1s, p90≤control+15s, and completed early60≤control+1pp. Late means A−F>60s, early <−60s; symmetric accuracy includes ±60s.

**Predictions.** ML1: C-30 gain over forecast+uniform is <1pp on each measured test date, a descriptive low-opportunity hypothesis. ML2: U primary learner reduces Y60≥1pp against both uniform controls on both dates, subject to guards. ML3: static FREE reduces Y60≥3pp on day13, positive in each window, subject to guards. ML4: none of the three fixed learners for static FREE/LOCKS or measured U jointly matches same-world **C-30 forecast+uniform**, using the same E30 calibration-cost rule, in Y60 and symmetric accuracy. Test both measured dates/static day13 on own/common-completed cohorts; report coverage/horizon differences. ML5: for gain G=control Y60−model Y60 versus forecast+uniform (uniform in static), removing only the two state counts gives Gfull−Gnostate≥Gfull/2 when Gfull>0, on the same cohort/budget with guards. Diagnostic, no causal attribution.

**Kill.** Invalid timing/provenance stops before fitting. If ML2 and ML3 both fail, stop expanding this learner family and report no demonstrated gain in this pilot. Few dates preclude global/equivalence claims; an incident failure never licenses changing the split or training on it.
