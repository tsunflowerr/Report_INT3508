**A reproduced result does not certify the full promised protocol.** [ML-PILOT-SEALED.md](../claude-work/ML-PILOT-SEALED.md:3) says the Codex exact version was adopted unchanged. That version requires original source-trip groups, exclusion of calibration duplicates, and purging fit/calibration source-trip duplicates before fitting ([ML-PILOT-REVIEW-CODEX.md](ML-PILOT-REVIEW-CODEX.md:15)). The new seal does not reproduce those gates; extraction retains only a shortened rider identifier and contains no source-group recovery or duplicate purge ([ml_pilot_extract.py](../claude-work/ml_pilot_extract.py:29)). Actual source overlap is being audited separately. The implementation also omits the common-completed cohorts explicitly required by seal line 104: its ML4 loop compares own cohorts only (run.py 140–155). It reports horizon in the CSV but not policy coverage denominators or horizon differences in ML4 text. These are omissions, not arithmetic discrepancies.

**Equal E is equal calibration budget, not equal realized test quote cost.** At E30, measured U HGB mean extensions are 41.624/35.565 seconds on 15/16, versus same-world C30 forecast+uniform 36.717/31.952; static FREE HGB is 29.392 versus C30 30 seconds. Measured U cohorts are 145/200, versus C30 147/197; static FREE/LOCKS/C30 serve 807/792/772 of 864 requests. C30’s coverage trade-off remains visible. Test mean horizons likewise differ: static FREE HGB 692.941 versus C30 698.954 seconds; measured U HGB 853.323/902.008 versus C30 844.921/896.129. Quotations need this qualification.

Measured ML2 HGB versus forecast+uniform fails mean/p90 guards by +4.405/+21.783 seconds on 15 and +3.793/+21.679 on 16; the latter also adds 2 percentage points early60. Static FREE’s 1.239 pp gain fails the 3 pp target, p90 (+32.011 seconds) and early (+6.939 pp) guards. ML5’s equal gains mean equal Y60 counts, not identical predictions. ML1 is a descriptive zero-gain result, not equivalence or a guarantee. Clipping and rho scaling do not preserve quantile coverage guarantees.

**Three-sentence thesis interpretation:** In these saved simulated worlds, the fixed historical-profile baseline and dispatch commitment produced a stronger reliability/quote-cost trade-off than the three frozen learners using these simple initial-promise and fleet-state inputs. This supports a bounded comparison of Fielbaum and Alonso-Mora’s two remedies—better predictions and fewer changes—but equal quote cost means the sealed calibration budget, with realized test costs, horizons, service coverage and common-completed checks reported separately. Few days, small test cohorts of 145–807 riders, fixed learners, simple features and simulated worlds preclude a general claim that fewer changes outperform prediction or that pooled-trip ML has no value.
